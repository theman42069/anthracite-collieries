Anthracite Collieries of Pennsylvania

In Memoriam

The men and boys who were killed working at the collieries on this map, as named in the state mine inspectors' yearly reports and in newspapers of the time. Names are spelled the way the records spelled them.

Not complete yet

This list is not everyone. Thousands of people died in these mines, and only some years' records have been put online so far.

    Know a name that's missing, or a better spelling? Please open an issue on GitHub with where it comes from.
